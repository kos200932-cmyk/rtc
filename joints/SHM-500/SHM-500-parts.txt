# SHM-500: adapter cup for the RI115-PH rotor on the DMRD-KB45 input shaft (level 2: interfaces,
# no chamfers / fillets / standard fasteners). Coordinates: the frame of DMRD-KB45 (x=0 - motor end of
# the input shaft), mm. Here the screws fit: the 6-M4 of the shaft end are on D57.5 (heads r 25.25..32.25)
# and the cup bore is D65 (r 32.5), so the heads are reached from inside the cup.
#   cup: bottom 6 mm on the shaft face, 6xM4 into the shaft, centring spigot D50 in the shaft bore
#   (left bore D50 x 14), rotor seat D74 x 32 (rotor bore 74), collar D80
#   torque: shaft -> 6xM4 -> cup -> rotor (RI115 peak 16 N*m)
#   rotor (Hall / board side outward): x -32..0; stator lamination x -27..0, potting to x 4
#   (6 mm clear of the housing boss D106 at x 10), board outward to x -35.1
#   cables: D44. The M4 heads have 0.25 mm to the cup bore - low-head screws at the design stage.

PART SHM-500-01_RotorCup
MOTION rotate
P -35   32.5
P -35   40       # collar D80 - axial stop of the rotor
P -32   40
P -32   37       # rotor seat D74 x 32
P 0     37
P 0     25       # bottom face on the shaft end face (r 25..32.5)
P 10    25       # centring spigot D50 in the left bore of the shaft (14 deep)
P 10    22       # bore D44 - cables
P -6    22
P -6    32.5     # bottom 6 thick; inside the cup bore D65 for the screw heads
#      x     dir  n   pcd    d    depth  angle
HOLES  -6    1    6   57.5   4.5  6      90       # 6 x D4.5 -> 6-M4 of the shaft end (D57.5)
PORT  shaft  0     1   role=on_shaft_end fit=50g6 fit_x=0..10 bolts=6xM4 pcd=57.5 ang=90
PORT  rotor  -32   -1  role=rotor_seat fit=74 fit_x=-32..0 collar=80
END

# ---- motor housing (stator holder, fixed) - centred on the reducer pilot D168h7 ---------------------
# Repeats the reducer mounting for the previous link on its flange: pilot D168h7 x -3..10, face at x=10.
# The reducer ring has 18 x D6.6 through holes (D180, from 90 deg every 20) and no threads on this face:
#   9 of them (90, 130, ...) - M6 in the housing flange: housing -> reducer, bolts from the output side;
#   9 of them (110, 150, ...) - D6.6 through the housing: previous link + housing + reducer, one bolt stack.
# Bore D168H7 only on the reducer pilot (x 24..40; 14 mm of wall to the outer pilot).
# Stator: seat D115 (wall 4, outer D123), shoulder D113.5 at x=0 (potting D113); clamp ring (part 03)
# from behind, 4 x M4 into the rear flange D132 x -38..-31 (D127.5). Oil plugs (x=27, D116) under the housing.
PART SHM-500-02_MotorHousing
MOTION fixed
P -38   57.5     # stator seat D115 (lamination x -27..0)
P -38   66       # rear flange D132 for the clamp-ring screws
P -31   66
P -31   61.5     # outer D123 - wall 4 over the stator
P -3    61.5
P -3    84       # pilot D168h7 for the previous link (as on the reducer)
P 10    84
P 10    95       # flange D190, link face at x=10
P 40    95
P 40    84       # bore D168H7 on the reducer pilot (x 27..40)
P 24    84
P 24    56.75    # D113.5 over the reducer boss D106; shoulder at x=0 - stop of the lamination
P 0     56.75
P 0     57.5
#       x     dir  n   pcd    size depth  angle  drillDepth
THREAD  40    -1   9   180    M6   12     90             # 9-M6: housing -> reducer (bolts from the output side)
THREAD  -38   1    4   127.5  M4   6      45     7       # 4-M4 for the clamp ring
#      x     dir  n   pcd    d    depth  angle
HOLES  40    -1   9   180    6.6  0      110      # 9 x D6.6: previous link + housing + reducer
PORT  base     10    -1  role=fixed fit=168h7 fit_x=-3..10 holes=9xD6.6 pcd=180 ang=110 mate=prev_link   # = reducer base, moved here
PORT  reducer  40    1   role=on_reducer fit=168H7 fit_x=27..40 bolts=9xM6 pcd=180 ang=90
PORT  stator   0     -1  role=stator_seat fit=115 fit_x=-27..0 shoulder_x=0
PORT  rear     -38   -1  role=rear_end bore=115 bolts=4xM4 pcd=127.5 ang=45
END

# ---- stator clamp ring (fixed) - axial stop of the stator from behind --------------------------------
# Lip D115 / D113.2 x -31.5..-27 on the lamination face at x=-27 (potting D113 - only 1 mm of the
# lamination is free, lip 0.9 mm: glue the stator as well), D104 behind (the board is inside D112.5 ->
# the clamp clears it radially from x -35.1), flange D132 x -41..-38 with 4 x M4 into the housing.
PART SHM-500-03_StatorClamp
MOTION fixed
P -41   52
P -41   66       # flange D132
P -38   66
P -38   57.5     # in the housing bore D115
P -27   57.5
P -27   56.6     # lip on the lamination face
P -35.5 56.6     # lip root ends before the flange line (no collinear edges at x=-38); board starts at x -35.1
P -35.5 52
#      x     dir  n   pcd    d    depth  angle
HOLES  -41   1    4   127.5  4.5  3      45       # 4 x D4.5 -> 4-M4 of the housing
PORT  housing  -38   1   role=on_housing bolts=4xM4 pcd=127.5 ang=45
PORT  stator   -27   1   role=stator_stop
END
