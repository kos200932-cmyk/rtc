# SHM-KB50: parts of the joint DMRD-KB50 + RI115-PH-KV40, flange layout (user, 2026-10-22).
# Coordinates: the frame of DMRD-KB50 (x=0 - motor end of the input shaft), mm. Level 2: interfaces only.
# Link interface: the previous link on the REDUCER side of the flange, bore D214H7 on the reducer ring D214h7;
# 12 x M8 DIN912 on D228 through the flange (heads on the motor side) into M8 of the link (D228: thread 2.5 mm from
# the link bore). Flange D250. Flange -> reducer: 12 x M8 DIN912 from the output side through the reducer 12 x D9 (D200)
# into M8 of the flange. The 6-M5 of the reducer ring (D200) are not used.
#
# Rotor: RI115 bore D74 is smaller than the shaft D75h7 and the 6-M5 of the shaft end (D65, DIN912 heads r 28.25..36.75)
# would lie under the rotor seat (r 37) - so the cup is a tube through the hollow input shaft (as SHM-60 / SHM-150),
# centred in both D55 bores, clamped by a flange on the output end with the shaft's 6-M5 there:
#   torque: shaft -> 6xM5 -> clamp flange -> 3xM4 -> cup -> rotor (RI115 peak 16 N*m)
#   axial: cup shoulder on the left shaft face (r 27.5..37) <- clamp -> flange on the right shaft face
#   rotor x -32..0 on the seat D74 (Hall side outward), stator lamination x -27..0, potting to x 4 (11 mm clear of
#   the reducer boss D125 at x 15), board to x -35.1. Cable passage D42.
# The clamp flange (D75 = shaft OD, x 118..121) and its M5 heads turn with the INPUT shaft: the next link needs a
# central recess >= D78 x 9 on the output face.
