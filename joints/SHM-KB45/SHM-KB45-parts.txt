# Link interface (NEW): the previous link sits on the REDUCER side of the flange, its bore D190H7 centred on the outer
# surface of the reducer ring D190; 18 x M6 DIN912 on D202 through the flange (heads on the motor side) into M6 of the
# link. D202 = the smallest circle: thread M6 2.5 mm from the link bore D190. Flange D220 (4 mm beyond the D10 heads).
# Flange -> reducer: 18 x M6 DIN912 from the output side through the reducer 18 x D6.6 (D180) into M6 of the flange.
# Rotor cup and stator clamp - as in SHM-500 (same reducer and motor).
