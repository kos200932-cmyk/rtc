# SHM-KB32: parts of the joint DMRD-KB32 + RI70-KV95, flange layout (user, 2026-10-22).
# Coordinates: the frame of DMRD-KB32 (x=0 - motor end of the input shaft), mm. Level 2: interfaces only.
# Link interface: the previous link on the REDUCER side of the flange, bore D142H7 on the reducer ring D142h7;
# 12 x M5 DIN912 on D152 through the flange (heads on the motor side) into M5 of the link (D152: thread 2.5 mm from the
# link bore). Flange D170. Flange -> reducer: 12 x M5 DIN912 from the output side through the reducer 12 x D5.5 (D132)
# into M5 of the flange. The 4-M4 of the reducer ring (D132, 45 deg) are not used.
#
# Rotor: RI70 bore D38 is smaller than the shaft D45h7 and the 6-M3 of the shaft end are on D40.5 - the cup is a tube
# through the hollow input shaft (as SHM-150), centred in both D36 bores, clamped by a flange on the output end:
#   torque: shaft -> 6xM3 -> clamp flange -> 3xM3 -> cup -> rotor (RI70 peak 2.68 N*m)
#   axial: cup disc D45 x -2..0 on the left shaft face (r 18..22.5) <- clamp -> flange on the right shaft face
#   motor placed 2 mm out (PLACE motor -2): rotor x -17..-2 on the seat D38 between the collar and the disc,
#   stator lamination x -15..-2, windings to x 1.5 (5.5 mm clear of the reducer boss D80 at x 7), leads to x -46.5.
#   Cable passage D28.
# The clamp flange (D45, x 65.5..68.5) turns with the INPUT shaft: the next link needs a central recess >= D50 x 4.

PART SHM-KB32-01_RotorCup
MOTION rotate
P -20   14
P -20   21       # collar D42 - axial stop of the rotor
P -17   21
P -17   19       # rotor seat D38 x 15
P -2    19
P -2    22.5     # disc D45 x 2 - stop of the rotor and shoulder on the left shaft face
P 0     22.5
P 0     18       # tube D36 through the shaft (fits both D36 bores)
P 65.5  18
P 65.5  14       # bore D28 - cable passage
#       x     dir  n   pcd    size depth  angle
THREAD  65.5  -1   3   32.5   M3   6      0       # 3-M3 in the tube end for the clamp flange (D32.5)
PORT  shaft  0     1   role=fit_in_shaft fit=36g6 fit_x=0..12,41.5..65.5 shoulder_x=0
PORT  rotor  -17   -1  role=rotor_seat fit=38 fit_x=-17..-2 collar=42
END

HOLES  68.5  -1   6   40.5   3.4  0      90       # 6 x D3.4 -> 6-M3 of the shaft end (D40.5)

PORT  shaft  65.5  -1  role=on_shaft_end bolts=6xM3 pcd=40.5 ang=90
END

# ---- flange + motor housing (one part, fixed) -------------------------------------------------------
# Bore D122H7 on the reducer pilot (x 17..24; relief from x 15), face x=24 on the reducer ring D142 (input side).
# Flange D170 x 9..24 (15 thick: M5 thread 10 + drill, 6 mm web in front of the pilot relief).
# The reducer boss D80 is larger than the stator seat D76: D83 over the boss (x 4..15, outer D90), shoulder D74 at
# x -2..4 (stop of the lamination), stator seat D76 (wall 3.5, outer D83), rear flange D92 x -24..-17 for the stator
# clamp (4 x M3 on D85). Reducer oil plugs (x=17, D88) are under the flange.
PART SHM-KB32-03_FlangeHousing
MOTION fixed
P -24   38       # stator seat D76 (lamination x -15..-2)
P -24   46       # rear flange D92

P -17   41.5     # outer D83 - wall 3.5 over the stator
P 2     41.5
P 2     45       # outer D90 over the reducer boss
P 9     45
P 9     85       # link flange D170
P 24    85
P 24    61       # bore D122H7 on the reducer pilot
P 15    61
P 15    41.5     # D83 over the reducer boss D80
P 4     41.5
P 4     37       # shoulder D74 - stop of the lamination at x=-2
P -2    37

#       x     dir  n   pcd    size depth  angle  drillDepth
THREAD  24    -1   12  132    M5   10     90     12.5   # 12-M5: reducer -> flange (DIN912 from the output side)
THREAD  -24   1    4   85     M3   6      45     7      # 4-M3 for the stator clamp
#      x     dir  n   pcd    d    depth  angle

END
