# SHM-KB17: parts of the joint DMRD-KB17 + RI70-KV95 (J7, 2026-10-24). Coordinates: the frame of DMRD-KB17 (x=0 - motor
# end of the input shaft), mm. Level 2: interfaces only (no chamfers / fillets / standard fasteners).
# KB17 input shaft D25h7 has no axial threads at its ends: the motor adapter is clamped by 3 x M3 RADIAL through the
# shaft at x=2.5 (catalog) - here the rotor cup sits ON the shaft end D25 (x 0..6) and the 3 radial M3 go through the
# cup wall into the shaft (radial holes are not modelled in revolved parts - drill / tap them on assembly).
#   torque: shaft -> 3 x M3 radial (+ fit 25H7/k6, Loctite) -> cup -> rotor (RI70 peak 2.68 N*m; J7 needs ~0.1 N*m)
#   rotor x -15..0 on the seat D38 (Hall side outward), stator lamination x -13..0, windings to x 3.5 (3 mm clear of the
#   reducer boss D52 at x 6.5), leads to x -44.5. Cables (J7 D16): through the cup bore D19 = the shaft bore D19.
# Housing: link interface as SHM-60 (pilot D94h7 x 1.5..6.5, flange D110 x 6.5..21.5, 12 x D4.5 on D102, M4 from the
# reducer side - heads r 47.5..54.5, clear of the KB17 ring r 40); on the reducer: bore D67H7 on its pilot x 16.5..21.5,
# face x=21.5 on the ring; reducer -> housing: 12 x M3 DIN912 from the output side through the reducer D3.5 (D74) into M3
# of the housing (thread 4.5; heads on the reducer output-side face at r 34.25..39.75, outside the output shaft D62).
# Stator seat D76, shoulder D74 at x=0, clamp ring from behind (part 03) as SHM-60.
